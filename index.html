FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The things people want to know before they send an enquiry — pricing, ownership, timelines and how working across time zones actually goes.

What does Spark Point Infotech do?

Spark Point Infotech is a software development company. It builds websites, custom web applications, and iOS and Android mobile apps for small and mid-sized businesses, and provides UI/UX design and ongoing maintenance. The company works with clients in India, Germany and the Netherlands.

How much does a website or web application cost?

Spark Point Infotech quotes a fixed price for every project, but only after a paid discovery phase. Discovery produces a written scope, a firm price and a dated schedule, typically within two weeks. Pricing depends on the number of screens, integrations and user roles.

How long does a software project take?

It depends on scope. Spark Point Infotech commits to a schedule only once that scope is known. Rather than quote a number that cannot hold, the paid discovery phase produces a dated, phase-by-phase schedule for your specific project. Work then runs in two-week increments.

Do you work with companies in Germany and the Netherlands?

Yes. Spark Point Infotech works with businesses in Germany and the Netherlands alongside its Indian clients. Contracts and correspondence are in English, invoicing supports EUR, and we follow EU data protection requirements for projects handling EU personal data.

How do you handle the time difference with Europe?

IST is 3.5 to 4.5 hours ahead of European CET, leaving a working overlap of roughly five hours every afternoon. All calls and reviews are scheduled inside that window, so European clients never take a meeting outside business hours.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

The client owns all of it. Full ownership of source code, designs and IP is transferred on final payment, and we hand over the repository, credentials and documentation at launch.

Is the software GDPR compliant?

Spark Point Infotech builds to GDPR requirements for every client with EU users — covering EU-region hosting, signed DPA, consent handling, data export routines, and documented retention periods.

What technologies do you build with?

We choose technology per project rather than fitting every client to one stack. The choice is made during discovery and written into the scope document before any code is built.

What happens after the software launches?

Every project includes thirty days of post-launch support at no extra cost. After that, clients can move to a monthly care plan covering security patching, dependency updates, uptime monitoring, and backup verification.

How do we start a project with Spark Point Infotech?

Send the project details through the enquiry form on this page or message the team on WhatsApp. We reply within one business day and arrange a call.
